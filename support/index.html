Help with the app

For StoreCustomer on iPhone and iPad.

If something did not work, or you have a question about an order or your account, write to us. A person reads this address.

Email support

What to include

  1. iPhone or iPad, and what you were doing (search, cart, sign-in, or an order).
  2. What you expected, and what happened instead.
  3. The email address on the account, if the question is about an order or a sign-in.

Do not send your password.

Account

To ask for your account to be deleted, follow the steps on the delete account page. The privacy policy explains what the app does with information.

راسل الدعم

ما الذي يُفيد تضمينه

  1. هل الجهاز iPhone أم iPad، وما الذي كنت تفعله (بحث، سلة، تسجيل دخول، أو طلب).
  2. ما الذي توقعت حدوثه، وما الذي حدث بدلًا منه.
  3. البريد المسجّل في الحساب، إذا كان السؤال عن طلب أو عن تسجيل الدخول.

لا ترسل كلمة المرور.
